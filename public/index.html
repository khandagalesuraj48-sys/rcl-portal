User management

Users & Access

Manage users and control their access to modules and sites. Access is separate on every site; switched off = cannot sign in.

A new user gets a one-time password: at his first sign-in he sets his own. A new one-time password ends his sign-ins.

Site management

Sites

Every site of every module. Closed = switched off: nobody can open it. A closed site can be deleted – its data stays in its own database.

New site

Mechanical for a new site – the same app, its own database. Nothing of another site is copied and no user is made; afterwards you give the site to people under Users & Access.
